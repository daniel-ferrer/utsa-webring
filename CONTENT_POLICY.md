# Content policy

utsa-webring links to members' personal sites. To stay in the ring, a site must not contain or link to:

- Sexual or graphic violent content
- Harassment, hate speech, or content attacking people for who they are
- Personal information about others without their consent (doxxing)
- Illegal content, piracy, or instructions to harm people or systems
- Malware, phishing, cryptomining scripts, or deceptive downloads
- Crypto, MLM or get-rich-quick pitches, or pages that are mostly ads or affiliate links
- Impersonation of another person or organization, including pages that look like official UTSA pages

Personal opinions, including political ones, are fine on a personal site as long as they don't fall into the list
above. Maintainers moderate; they don't edit.

## Reporting

- Public: open a [report](https://github.com/daniel-ferrer/utsa-webring/issues/new?template=report.yml).
- Private: email **report@utsa-webring.com**. Use this for anything sensitive. You never have to report publicly.

## What happens next

1. A maintainer reviews the report, usually within a week.
2. If the report is credible, the entry is removed right away in a pull request, and the member is told why.
3. The member can fix the issue and join again. Removal is a pause, not a punishment.

Entries whose domain has expired or changed hands are removed without discussion.

Members can leave at any time by asking to be removed.
